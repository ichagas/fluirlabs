Pizzaria: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026 · Applies to the Pizzaria apps for iOS and Android (bundle id com.fluirlabs.games.pizzaria) and to the web version.

In short. Pizzaria has no accounts, no sign-up and no server of ours. Your progress stays on your device. We do not run analytics. The apps show ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some information about your device to do that. You choose whether those ads are personalised.

1. Who we are

Pizzaria is made by Fluir Labs (“we”, “us”). If you have a question about this policy or your information, write to fluirgames@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

The game saves everything it needs in the storage of your device: your progress (nights played, money, upgrades, staff, trophies), your settings (volume, language, vibration, accessibility), and two small counters used for ads (when the last full-screen ad was shown and how many reward videos you watched today). None of this is sent to us. We do not receive it and cannot see it.

  • Save code. The game can show you a text code of your save so you can move it to another device. It leaves your device only if you copy or share it yourself.
  • Device backups. Your phone’s own backup (iCloud, Google) may include the game’s data. That is controlled by your device settings and the operating system’s provider, not by us.
  • The web version stores the same information in your browser (local storage and IndexedDB). It shows no ads and sets no cookies of ours.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device.

3. Ads and Google AdMob

The iOS and Android apps show two kinds of ads, served by Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between nights, and an optional reward video you can watch to get a free restock or a discount. The web version shows no ads.

Google acts as an independent controller of that information. We do not receive it, other than summarised ad performance and earnings reports. Google’s rules are at policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Your choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and similar regions. The first time you open the app, Google’s consent form asks whether Google and its partners may use your data for personalised ads. You can change your answer at any time: Settings in the game, then “Ad privacy choices” (shown wherever the law requires it).
  • iOS. The app asks permission before it tracks you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you say no, ads can still appear, but are not based on tracking. You can change it in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ads personalisation, in Settings, Google, Ads.
  • California and other US states. You can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising through the controls above and at google.com/settings/ads.
  • You can always play without watching reward videos. They are optional.

5. Permissions the apps use

Internet access (for ads), vibration (for the game’s haptics), keeping the screen awake while you play a night, and, on Android, Google’s advertising ID permission. The apps do not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

6. Children

Pizzaria is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age set by the law of your country for giving consent, such as 16 in parts of the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will help. Parents can use the controls in section 4 and their device’s family settings.

7. Who we share information with

We do not collect your personal information, so we have nothing of that kind to sell or share. Google (AdMob) and the app stores (Apple, Google Play) process information under their own policies, for example to deliver the app, purchases and crash reports.

8. How long information is kept

Game data lives on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Google keeps ads information according to its own retention policy.

9. International transfers

Google processes ads data in many countries, including outside the EEA and the UK. Google describes the safeguards it uses (such as standard contractual clauses) in its privacy and data processing terms.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Brazil’s LGPD or US state laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export or object to the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal information about you, most of these are already in your hands: your game data is on your device. For information Google holds about your ads profile, use My Ad Center or write to us and we will point you to the right place. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD).

11. Security

Game data stays in your device’s protected app storage. We use no server of our own, so there is none to be breached. Ads are delivered over encrypted connections.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app does with information, we will update this page and its date, and, if the change is significant, tell you in the app.
