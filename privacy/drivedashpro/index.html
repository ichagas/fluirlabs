Drive Dash Pro: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · Applies to the Drive Dash Pro apps for iOS and Android (bundle id com.fluirlabs.games.drivedashpro).

In short. Drive Dash Pro has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server of ours, and it does not use the internet. Everything it saves stays on your device. Nothing is sent to us unless you choose to send us a play log yourself.

1. Who we are

Drive Dash Pro is made by Fluir Labs (“we”, “us”). If you have a question about this policy or your information, write to fluirgames@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

The game saves everything it needs in the storage of your device. None of this is sent to us.

  • Progress and settings: career results, stars, best times, cash, cars, parts, your chosen driver, and settings such as volume and vibration.
  • Ghosts: recordings of the car positions of your best runs, used to race against yourself in Time Attack.
  • Play log: when races start and finish, the track and car, times, and errors the game ran into. It carries a random player number made on your device, not your name or any account.
  • Device backups. Your phone’s own backup (iCloud, Google) may include the game’s data. That is controlled by your device settings, not by us.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device.

3. When information leaves your device

Only when you tap Send log in the game’s settings. Your phone’s share menu opens and you choose the app and the recipient (for example, an email to us). If you send us a log, we use it only to find bugs and balance the game, we do not sell or share it, and we delete it when you ask.

4. Permissions the apps use

Vibration (for the game’s haptics). The apps do not use internet access and do not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

5. Children

Drive Dash Pro is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. It has no ads, no purchases, no chat and collects no personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information in a play log, write to us and we will delete it.

6. Who we share information with

No one. We do not collect your personal information, so we have nothing to sell or share. The app stores (Apple, Google Play) process information under their own policies, for example to deliver the app and crash reports.

7. How long information is kept

Game data lives on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. A play log you send us is kept only while we work on the problems it shows, and deleted when you ask.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Brazil’s LGPD or US state laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information. Your game data is on your device, so these are in your hands; for a play log you sent us, write to us. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD).

9. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app does with information (for example, adding purchases or online features), we will update this page and its date before that version ships, and, if the change is significant, tell you in the app.
