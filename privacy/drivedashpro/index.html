Drive Dash Pro: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Applies to the Drive Dash Pro apps for iOS and Android (bundle id com.fluirlabs.games.drivedashpro).

In short. Drive Dash Pro has no accounts. Your progress stays on your device. The app sends us anonymous usage counts (such as which race number players reach) that cannot be linked to you, and you can turn them off. Online races connect two phones directly, through a small server of ours that stores nothing. The game is free with ads served by Google AdMob, and Google may collect some information about your device to show them. The one-time Full Game purchase is handled by the app store; we never see your payment details. Nothing else is sent to us unless you choose to send us a play log yourself.

1. Who we are

Drive Dash Pro is made by Fluir Labs (“we”, “us”). If you have a question about this policy or your information, write to fluirgames@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

The game saves everything it needs in the storage of your device. None of this is sent to us.

  • Progress and settings: career results, stars, best times, cash, cars, parts, your chosen driver, and settings such as volume and vibration.
  • Ghosts: recordings of the car positions of your best runs, used to race against yourself in Time Attack.
  • Full Game and ad counters: whether you own the Full Game, when the last between-race ad was shown and how many reward ads you watched today.
  • Play log: when races start and finish, the track and car, times, and errors the game ran into. It carries a random player number made on your device, not your name or any account.
  • Device backups. Your phone’s own backup (iCloud, Google) may include the game’s data. That is controlled by your device settings, not by us.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device. A Full Game you bought comes back with RESTORE PURCHASES.

3. Ads and Google AdMob

The game shows two kinds of ads, served by Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between some races, only for players who don’t own the Full Game, and an optional reward ad anyone can choose to watch for in-game cash. Ads in the game are limited to content rated PG.

4. Purchases

The Full Game is a one-time purchase sold through Google Play (and the App Store when the iOS version is released). The store handles the payment under its own policy. The game only asks the store whether you own the Full Game, so that it can open World 2 and turn off the between-race ads. We never see your name, card or other payment details.

5. Online races

When you race a friend online, the two phones connect directly to each other (peer to peer). To find each other they first contact our small signaling server, run on Cloudflare, which pairs them by the 4-letter room code and passes on the connection details. The game also asks public STUN servers run by Google and Cloudflare for your phone’s public address.

  • What is exchanged: the room code, network connection details (including your IP address), and race data such as car positions, the cars and drivers chosen and race results. There is no chat, no name and no account.
  • What we keep: nothing. A room lives only in the server’s memory and ends within 10 minutes. Your IP address is used to connect you and to limit how often one address can create or join rooms; it is not stored.
  • The other player: because the connection is direct, the other player’s device receives your IP address, as with any peer-to-peer game. Only race with people you know.

6. Anonymous usage counts

To find where the game is too hard or confusing, the app sends us anonymous counts of game events, for example “race 3 finished”, “retried Crate Alley”, “left a race on lap 2”, “saw the Full Game screen”, “Daily Challenge gold”, “hosted an online race”, or “came back 7 days after installing”. They are added up on your phone and sent a few times per session to our server on Cloudflare, which keeps only daily totals per platform (Android or iOS).

  • No player number, name, advertising ID, device model, location or race times are sent, and the server does not store your IP address (it is used only to limit how often one address can send). The totals cannot be linked to you.
  • They are off in test builds, and you can turn them off at any time: Settings, General, Anonymous counts.
  • Daily totals are deleted after 400 days.

7. Your choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and similar regions. The first time you open the game, Google’s consent form asks whether Google and its partners may use your data for personalised ads. You can change your answer at any time: Settings in the game, then “Ad choices” (shown wherever the law requires it).
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ads personalisation, in Settings, Google, Ads.
  • California and other US states. You can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising through the controls above and at google.com/settings/ads.
  • Reward ads are optional. You can always play without watching them, and the Full Game removes the between-race ads.

8. Play logs you send us

The play log described in section 2 leaves your device only when you tap Send playtest log in the game’s settings. Your phone’s share menu opens and you choose the app and the recipient (for example, an email to us). If you send us a log, we use it only to find bugs and balance the game, we do not sell or share it, and we delete it when you ask.

9. Permissions the apps use

Internet and network state (for ads, the store, online races and the anonymous counts), Google Play billing (for the Full Game), Google’s advertising ID permission on Android, and vibration (for the game’s haptics). The apps do not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

10. Children

Drive Dash Pro is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age set by the law of your country for giving consent, such as 16 in parts of the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, for example in a play log, write to us and we will delete it. Parents can use the controls in section 7 and their device’s family settings, including purchase approvals.

11. Who we share information with

We do not collect your personal information, so we have nothing of that kind to sell or share. Cloudflare runs our signaling and counts server as our service provider, under its own privacy policy. Google (AdMob, Google Play) and Apple process information under their own policies, for example to show ads, take payments, deliver the app and send crash reports.

12. How long information is kept

Game data lives on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. A play log you send us is kept only while we work on the problems it shows, and deleted when you ask. Anonymous daily totals are deleted after 400 days; online rooms are not kept at all. Google keeps ads information according to its own retention policy.

13. International transfers

Google processes ads data, and Cloudflare runs our server, in many countries, including outside the EEA and the UK. Both describe the safeguards they use (such as standard contractual clauses) in their privacy and data processing terms.

14. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Brazil’s LGPD or US state laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export or object to the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Your game data is on your device, so most of these are already in your hands; for a play log you sent us, write to us. For information Google holds about your ads profile, use My Ad Center. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD).

15. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app does with information, we will update this page and its date before that version ships, and, if the change is significant, tell you in the app.
