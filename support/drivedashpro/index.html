Drive Dash Pro: Support

For the Drive Dash Pro apps for iOS and Android, made by Fluir Labs.

Need help? Write to fluirgames@gmail.com. Tell us your phone or tablet model and what happened, and we will answer as soon as we can.

The Full Game

  • What it includes: World 2 (the Sky Docks: 5 tracks, their seasons and cup), the cars Zip and Comet, the grand final against Dot, and no ads between races. It is one purchase, not a subscription.
  • Buying it: tap the FULL GAME card on the career map, or go to Settings → FULL GAME.
  • New phone or reinstalled the game? Open Settings → FULL GAME and tap RESTORE PURCHASES, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.
  • Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google Play in your Play Store order history.

Playing

  • Steering: choose Buttons, Drag, Tilt or One thumb in Settings → GENERAL → Steering. With Tilt, tap Calibrate tilt in a race to set how you hold the device.
  • Easier driving: Settings → ACCESSIBILITY has steering assist, bigger buttons, auto-boost, reduced motion and a left-handed layout.
  • Slow on an older device? Settings → GENERAL → Graphics: pick a lower level. It applies from the next race.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. There are no accounts, and the game plays offline.

Ads and privacy

  • Players without the Full Game see an ad between some races. Watching an ad for extra cash is always your choice.
  • Where the law asks for it, Settings → GENERAL → AD CHOICES lets you change your ad consent at any time.
  • Read how the game handles your information in the Privacy Policy.
